Five ways into Archgen

Loading screens for archgenlabs.com, built from the brand's own net, die, Hairline figure and wafer map. Route is the pick: it now runs about 2.75 seconds and lands in the header. The other four each finish within 1.2 seconds. Every one skips on a tap and drops to a still frame for anyone who asks for less motion.

Route

Pin one lights and the AG net routes out of it, net by net. The logo holds for a beat, then the nets route back into pin one, and the red pin rises on an arc to the header and writes ARCHGEN LABS out of itself.

Gone at 2.77 s on desktop and 2.78 s on a phone, from a cold page.

Place

The hero's eight macros start piled in the middle and legalise into the exact floorplan the home page opens on. CORE2 takes the red edge, then the die rushes in and becomes the page.

Gone at 1.13 s on desktop and 1.13 s on a phone, from a cold page.

Stack

Your Hairline figure, Tape-out, unchanged. Package, die and net close on the kernel's own spring, with pin one as the one bright mark the whole way down.

Gone at 1.11 s on desktop and 1.09 s on a phone, from a cold page.

Close

CORE2's worst slack climbs from −0.185 to +0.003 ns, the run from the agent's log on the home page. Red while it fails, paper once it holds, then the dark stage closes into the one red pin.

Gone at 1.16 s on desktop and 1.16 s on a phone, from a cold page.

Expose

A wafer at the brand's 26 by 34 die proportion. The scanner steps across it in a serpentine, marks one die red, and the home page opens out of that die.

Gone at 1.15 s on desktop and 1.14 s on a phone, from a cold page.
